Title: Weekly Theory-Building Review

Please run the `theory-building-review` skill for our weekly repository audit. 
Scope the review to PRs merged in the last 3 days only.

Your goal is to reconstruct the current system theory from this recent evidence and identify any conceptual drift. Specifically look for:
- Duplicated concepts with different names
- Rules enforced by caller discipline instead of model boundaries
- Generic services owning business rules from multiple domains

Provide the standard Theory Summary, Strong Signals, Findings (prioritized by modification risk), and up to three small, concrete Action Items we can tackle next week.
